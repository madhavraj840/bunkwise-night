Darker (Screen Filter) vs Screen & Notification Dimmer¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

Darker is an established ad-free dimmer (1M+ downloads) whose paid features include Auto-On/Off, start at boot, going below 20%, darkening the navigation bar, custom colours, root mode and customisable notification buttons. In Screen & Notification Dimmer, the schedule, the full 0–100 range and the notification buttons are free, and any Pro feature can be tried free for 12 hours with an ad. Darker has root mode and fully customisable buttons, which I don't offer.

A note on bias

I make Screen & Notification Dimmer, so read this knowing that. Every fact about the other app comes from its own Google Play listing, checked on 25 November 2026. I have not claimed anything the listing does not say. If something is out of date, email support@bunkwise.in and I will correct it.

The listing says Darker can lower brightness to extremely low levels, has a colour filter, and uses Accessibility services to darken the screen. Paid features: Auto-On & Auto-Off, start at boot, lower brightness below 20%, darken navigation bar, custom filter colours, root mode, and up to three customisable notification buttons (±5%, ±10%, set levels, stop, pause, reset, colour filter). It reminds Xiaomi users to enable "Display pop-up window".

Where Darker is strong¶

  • No ads, with one clear purchase for extras.
  • Customisable notification buttons, with more choices than my fixed four.
  • Root mode for rooted phones.

Where Screen & Notification Dimmer is different¶

  • Free schedule (Auto-On/Off is paid in Darker).
  • The full darkness range is free (Darker charges for below 20%).
  • Free notification buttons: −10, +10 and OFF.
  • Edge slider, Auto-Dim and per-app levels.
  • Emergency off by shake or volume buttons.
  • More recent update: 21 November 2026.

Which should you choose?¶

Pick Darker if you are rooted or want to design your own notification buttons. Pick Screen & Notification Dimmer if you want the schedule and full range without paying, or the edge slider and Auto-Dim.

Frequently asked questions¶

Is Darker screen filter free?¶

The basic filter is free with no ads. Its listing lists Auto-On/Off, start at boot, below 20%, navigation bar dimming, custom colours, root mode and custom notification buttons as paid.

Alternative to Darker with a free schedule?¶

Screen & Notification Dimmer has a free bedtime schedule and timer.
