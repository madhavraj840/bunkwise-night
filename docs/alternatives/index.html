Android screen dimmer apps compared (2026)¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

Any overlay dimmer will make an Android screen darker than its lowest brightness. The real differences are in the details: whether it can dim the notification shade and status bar, whether the schedule is free, how you switch it off in the dark, and whether it has ads. Screen & Notification Dimmer is the pick if you want the notification bar dimmed, an edge slider, content-aware Auto-Dim and a free schedule. Penumbra is a good pick if you want no ads at all, and Twilight if you mainly want a blue light filter.

A note on bias

I make Screen & Notification Dimmer, so read this knowing that. Every fact about the other app comes from its own Google Play listing, checked on 25 November 2026. I have not claimed anything the listing does not say. If something is out of date, email support@bunkwise.in and I will correct it.

How to choose a screen dimmer¶

  1. Does the notification shade stay bright? Plain overlays can't cover it. If pulling the shade down at night bothers you, choose an app that uses an Accessibility overlay for system areas (here is why).
  2. Can you switch it off blind? Look for shake or a hardware-button escape, and a level cap so the screen can't go fully black.
  3. Is the schedule free? It is the feature that makes a dimmer effortless, and several apps charge for it.
  4. Is it still maintained? Android changes overlay rules almost every year. An app last updated long ago is more likely to break on a new phone.
  5. What permissions does it want? Only "Display over other apps" is needed for basic dimming. Accessibility should be optional and explained.

Frequently asked questions¶

What is the best screen dimmer app for Android?¶

It depends on what you need. Screen & Notification Dimmer is strongest if you want the notification shade and status bar dimmed, an edge slider, Auto-Dim and a free schedule. Penumbra suits people who want no ads at all. Twilight suits people who mainly want a blue light filter.

Which screen dimmer can dim the notification bar?¶

Screen & Notification Dimmer (Pro, or 12 hours free with an ad) and, according to their listings, the premium tiers of Night screen and Super Dim. They all use an Accessibility overlay.

Is there a free screen dimmer with a schedule?¶

Yes. Screen & Notification Dimmer and Penumbra both list a free schedule.
