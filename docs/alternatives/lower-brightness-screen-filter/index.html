Lower Brightness Screen Filter vs Screen & Notification Dimmer¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

Lower Brightness Screen Filter is a basic, popular dimmer (5M+ downloads): set a percentage from 0 to 100, with auto start after reboot and a notification icon. Its listing shows "Contains ads" and was last updated in May 2026. Screen & Notification Dimmer adds a free schedule and timer, working notification buttons, an emergency off and, in Pro, dimming of the notification shade, and it only shows ads you choose to watch.

A note on bias

I make Screen & Notification Dimmer, so read this knowing that. Every fact about the other app comes from its own Google Play listing, checked on 25 November 2026. I have not claimed anything the listing does not say. If something is out of date, email support@bunkwise.in and I will correct it.

The listing lists: reducing brightness below the system minimum, a 0–100% level, auto start after reboot, a brightness icon in the notification bar that opens the app, support for on-screen navigation bars, a small app size and support for all Android versions.

Where it is strong¶

  • Very simple: one number, nothing to learn.
  • Small and widely used.

Where Screen & Notification Dimmer is different¶

  • Control without opening the app: −10, +10 and OFF on the notification, plus a tile.
  • Free schedule and timer.
  • Emergency off and a 97% cap, so you can't get stuck in the dark.
  • Notification shade dimming, edge slider and Auto-Dim in Pro.
  • No interruptions: ads only when you tap "Watch an ad".

Frequently asked questions¶

Does Lower Brightness have a schedule?¶

Its listing mentions auto start after reboot but no schedule or timer.

A Lower Brightness alternative without pop-up ads?¶

Screen & Notification Dimmer only shows rewarded ads when you tap "Watch an ad" to unlock a Pro feature; they never appear while you are dimming.
