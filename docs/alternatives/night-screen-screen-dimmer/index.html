Night screen: Screen Dimmer vs Screen & Notification Dimmer¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

Night screen is one of the most downloaded dimmers on Google Play (10M+, rated 4.4). Its free version is a simple one-slider dimmer with a blue light filter. Its listing puts the schedule, status bar dimming ("Advanced dimming"), the widget and the Quick Settings tile in the premium tier. In Screen & Notification Dimmer, the schedule and an on/off tile are free. Status bar dimming is Pro, but one short ad unlocks it for 12 hours. It also adds an edge slider, Auto-Dim and per-app levels.

A note on bias

I make Screen & Notification Dimmer, so read this knowing that. Every fact about the other app comes from its own Google Play listing, checked on 25 November 2026. I have not claimed anything the listing does not say. If something is out of date, email support@bunkwise.in and I will correct it.

The listing describes a lightweight dimmer: a dimming filter below the system minimum, a blue light filter, a single slider with a quick pause in the notification, automatic setting of system brightness to minimum (restored afterwards), and an optional keep-screen-on. Night screen + (premium) adds Advanced dimming over the status bar and notifications through Accessibility, an auto start and stop timer, a home screen widget and a Quick Settings tile.

Where Night screen is strong¶

  • Proven at scale: more than 10 million downloads and a 4.4 rating are hard to argue with.
  • It sets system brightness to minimum for you and restores it afterwards. That is a nice touch.
  • Home screen widget (premium). I don't have a widget.

Where Screen & Notification Dimmer is different¶

  • Free schedule and a free tile. Both are premium in Night screen's listing.
  • Status bar and shade dimming without paying: watch one ad for 12 hours, or buy Pro.
  • Finer notification controls: −10, +10, AUTO and OFF, against a quick pause.
  • Edge slider, Auto-Dim and per-app levels. Night screen's listing doesn't mention any of them.
  • Emergency off by shake or volume buttons.
  • Ads only when you ask: rewarded ads that play when you tap "Watch an ad", never while you are dimming.

Which should you choose?¶

If you want the most-used, simplest option and don't mind ads or a premium upgrade, Night screen is a safe choice. If you want the schedule free, want to try notification-bar dimming before paying, or want the edge slider and Auto-Dim, try Screen & Notification Dimmer. It is newer and smaller, but it is updated often; the latest release was on 21 November 2026.

Frequently asked questions¶

Is Night screen free?¶

The basic dimmer is free. Its listing shows "Contains ads" and "In-app purchases", and puts the schedule, Advanced dimming, widget and tile in Night screen + (premium).

What is a good alternative to Night screen?¶

Screen & Notification Dimmer has a free schedule and tile, and adds notification-bar dimming (Pro, or 12 hours free with an ad), an edge slider, Auto-Dim and per-app levels.
