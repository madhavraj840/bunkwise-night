Night Screen: Ultra Dim Mode vs Screen & Notification Dimmer¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer

Night Screen: Ultra Dim Mode (500K+ downloads) combines a dimmer with a blue light reading mode, an RGB custom colour filter and an auto schedule from sunset to morning. Screen & Notification Dimmer focuses on darkness and control: notification buttons, emergency off, the notification shade dimmed too (Pro), an edge slider and Auto-Dim. Choose the first for colour play, and the second for control in the dark.

A note on bias

I make Screen & Notification Dimmer, so read this knowing that. Every fact about the other app comes from its own Google Play listing, checked on 25 November 2026. I have not claimed anything the listing does not say. If something is out of date, email support@bunkwise.in and I will correct it.

The listing describes several modes: Dim Light (below the phone minimum), a blue light filter "Reading Mode", a customisable RGB screen filter with a colour picker, and Auto Schedule that enables the screen at a sunset time you set and disables it in the morning.

Where it is strong¶

  • Colour customisation with a full RGB picker.
  • Several modes ready to tap.

Where Screen & Notification Dimmer is different¶

  • Notification shade and status bar dimming (Pro, or 12 hours with an ad).
  • Notification buttons and tile to adjust without opening the app.
  • Emergency off and a 97% cap.
  • Edge slider, Auto-Dim and per-app levels.
  • Updated more recently: 21 November 2026, against 25 May 2026.
