Penumbra – Screen Dimmer vs Screen & Notification Dimmer¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

Penumbra is a well-made, free dimmer with no ads and no paywall. Its listing also highlights Tasker control, TalkBack support and seven languages. Screen & Notification Dimmer is the alternative if you want things Penumbra does not list: dimming the status bar and notification shade, an edge slider inside any app, content-aware Auto-Dim, per-app levels and a Volume Up + Down emergency off. If "no ads, ever" matters most to you, Penumbra is the honest choice.

A note on bias

I make Screen & Notification Dimmer, so read this knowing that. Every fact about the other app comes from its own Google Play listing, checked on 25 November 2026. I have not claimed anything the listing does not say. If something is out of date, email support@bunkwise.in and I will correct it.

According to its listing, Penumbra has a dial that goes below the system minimum, Extra Dim integration for going deeper, a continuous warm (amber) slider, a Quick Settings tile, a notification toggle, presets that save dim and warmth together, an auto schedule, an auto-off timer (15, 30 or 60 minutes), start on boot, shake to disable, Tasker and Automate broadcasts, and full TalkBack support. Its privacy section says it has no ads and no advertising ID, with anonymous crash and usage reporting only.

Where Penumbra is strong¶

  • Completely free, with no ads or paywall at all.
  • Automation through Tasker and Automate intents. I don't offer this yet.
  • Accessibility and languages: TalkBack support and seven languages are listed.
  • A clear note in the listing about overlays not saving battery on LCD screens, which I appreciate.

Where Screen & Notification Dimmer is different¶

  • The notification shade and status bar get dimmed. Penumbra's listing describes an overlay over apps and doesn't mention system areas. How this works.
  • Edge slider: change darkness by sliding along the screen edge, inside any app. Details.
  • Auto-Dim reacts to bright pages as they appear. Details.
  • Per-app levels, so YouTube can be darker than your reading app.
  • Volume Up + Volume Down emergency off, as well as shake.
  • Notification buttons for −10 and +10, not only a toggle.

Which should you choose?¶

Choose Penumbra if you want a free dimmer with no ads at all, or Tasker control. Choose Screen & Notification Dimmer if the bright notification shade is what bothers you, or you want the edge slider, Auto-Dim or per-app levels. Both are actively maintained; both were updated in November 2026.

Frequently asked questions¶

Is Penumbra free?¶

Its Google Play listing says it is free with no ads and no paywall.

Does Penumbra dim the notification bar?¶

Its listing does not mention dimming the status bar or notification shade. Screen & Notification Dimmer can, using its optional Accessibility service.

Penumbra or Screen & Notification Dimmer?¶

Penumbra for zero ads and Tasker; Screen & Notification Dimmer for notification-bar dimming, the edge slider, Auto-Dim and per-app levels.
