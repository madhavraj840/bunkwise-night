Super Dim vs Screen & Notification Dimmer¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

Super Dim is built for people with photophobia and strong light sensitivity. It dims below the minimum, can adjust automatically to ambient light, has a notification control and shake to turn off, and in premium extends the filter over the status bar, shade and navigation buttons. Screen & Notification Dimmer overlaps a lot, and adds a free schedule, an edge slider, content-aware Auto-Dim, per-app levels and a volume-button escape.

A note on bias

I make Screen & Notification Dimmer, so read this knowing that. Every fact about the other app comes from its own Google Play listing, checked on 25 November 2026. I have not claimed anything the listing does not say. If something is out of date, email support@bunkwise.in and I will correct it.

According to the listing, Super Dim reduces brightness below the device minimum, can be applied manually or adjust automatically to ambient light, offers a filter colour, a notification control and shake to turn off. Premium uses the Accessibility service to extend the filter over the status bar, notification shade and navigation buttons, and to take screenshots without the filter.

Where Super Dim is strong¶

  • Designed around light sensitivity, and it says so clearly.
  • Ambient-light automation using the light sensor.
  • Clean screenshots without the filter (premium). I don't have this on Android yet.

Where Screen & Notification Dimmer is different¶

  • Auto-Dim follows content, not the room. In a dark bedroom the room is constant; what changes is the page. How it works.
  • Free bedtime schedule and timer.
  • Edge slider and per-app levels.
  • Volume Up + Down escape, as well as shake.
  • System-area dimming unlockable free for 12 hours with one ad.

Frequently asked questions¶

Is Super Dim good for photophobia?¶

Super Dim's listing says it is built for people with photophobia and severe light sensitivity. Neither app is a medical device.

Super Dim vs Screen & Notification Dimmer: what is the difference in automatic dimming?¶

Super Dim adjusts to room light from the sensor; Screen & Notification Dimmer's Auto-Dim adjusts to how bright the on-screen content is.
