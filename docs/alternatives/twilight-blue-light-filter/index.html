Twilight vs Screen & Notification Dimmer: filter or dimmer?¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

Twilight and Screen & Notification Dimmer solve different problems. Twilight is a blue light filter: it warms the screen gradually, following your local sunset and sunrise. Screen & Notification Dimmer is a dimmer: it makes the screen darker than its minimum, with an optional red or amber tint. If your screen feels too bright, choose the dimmer. If it feels too blue, choose Twilight. Some people use both.

A note on bias

I make Screen & Notification Dimmer, so read this knowing that. Every fact about the other app comes from its own Google Play listing, checked on 25 November 2026. I have not claimed anything the listing does not say. If something is out of date, email support@bunkwise.in and I will correct it.

According to its listing, Twilight filters blue light after sunset with a soft red filter whose strength follows the sun cycle for your location. It can lower the backlight below the normal controls, tints dark areas such as the status bar, filters notifications and the lock screen with an optional Accessibility service, syncs with Wear OS, supports Tasker, and can control Philips Hue lights. It uses location to find sunset times.

Where Twilight is strong¶

  • Sunset-aware timing that shifts through the year without you touching it.
  • A huge, long-standing user base and a high rating.
  • Extras: Wear OS, Philips Hue and Tasker.

Where Screen & Notification Dimmer is different¶

  • Darkness first. A plain black layer that keeps colours true, useful for films and photos, with tints as an option.
  • Edge slider and notification buttons to change darkness quickly, in the moment.
  • Auto-Dim reacts to bright content, not to the time of day.
  • Emergency off by shake or volume buttons.
  • No location permission. The schedule uses the times you set.

Using both¶

A sensible night setup is Twilight (or Android's Night Light) for warmth and Screen & Notification Dimmer for darkness. Keep the dimmer's filter on Black when you do, so the two tints don't stack into something too red to read.

Frequently asked questions¶

Is a blue light filter the same as a screen dimmer?¶

No. A blue light filter changes colour; a dimmer reduces brightness. Twilight is mainly a filter; Screen & Notification Dimmer is mainly a dimmer.

Can I use Twilight and a dimmer together?¶

Yes. Use Twilight for warmth and the dimmer for darkness, with the dimmer's colour set to Black.
