Android screen dimmer: go below minimum brightness with an overlay¶

A 2026 guide to dimming an Android phone beyond its lowest brightness setting, and how Night Mode on Google Play does it.

Quick answer¶

To make an Android screen darker than its minimum brightness, use an overlay dimmer app. Install Night Mode on Google Play, allow “Display over other apps”, then pick a level such as 45% or drag the slider. The app draws a dark layer over the screen, so it goes darker than the brightness slider allows.

Get it on Google Play

Why is the lowest brightness still too bright?¶

Phone makers set a minimum brightness so the screen stays readable in daylight. In a dark bedroom that floor can still feel harsh, especially when reading in bed or sitting next to someone who is asleep. The system brightness slider cannot go lower, which is where an overlay helps.

How does an overlay screen dimmer work?¶

An overlay dimmer asks for Android’s “Display over other apps” permission and then draws a semi-transparent dark layer on top of the screen. Everything underneath, including apps, websites, video and the home screen, looks dimmer. Turning it off removes the layer straight away. Night Mode shows the level as a percentage darker than Android’s lowest brightness.

Night Mode vs Extra dim, brightness, dark mode and blue light filters¶

These settings are often mixed up. They solve different problems.

Method What it does Goes below minimum brightness?
Brightness slider Sets the display’s brightness within the range the phone allows No
Extra dim (built in) A system setting on many phones that dims beyond the slider. Availability and strength depend on your phone and Android version Yes, where available
Night Light / blue light filter Warms screen colours for the evening No
Dark mode Switches supported apps to dark colours No
Night Mode app Overlay dimmer with an adjustable level, notification buttons, Quick Settings tiles and a floating slider Yes, you choose how far

Night Mode and Extra dim can be a good pair. If your phone has Extra dim, try both and keep whichever feels better.

How to set up Night Mode on Android¶

  1. Install Screen Dimmer - Night Mode from Google Play and open it
  2. Allow “Display over other apps”. This is the only permission the dimmer needs. If Android shows “Screen overlay detected”, close other apps that draw over the screen and try again
  3. Choose a level. Tap 30%, 45%, 60% or 80%, or drag the slider. Higher means darker than Android’s lowest brightness
  4. Adjust from anywhere. Use the notification buttons (−10, +10, AUTO, OFF), the Quick Settings tiles, or the floating slider

See the User Guide for every control.

What if it gets too dark?¶

  • Shake the phone. This is on by default
  • Tap OFF in the Night Mode notification, or use the Quick Settings tile
  • Volume Up + Volume Down together, if you enabled the optional Accessibility service

Begin with 30% to get a feel for the levels, then go darker.
