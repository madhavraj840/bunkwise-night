Overlay dimming vs real brightness: what actually happens¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer

The system brightness slider changes how much light the panel produces. An overlay dimmer such as Screen & Notification Dimmer lays a semi-transparent dark layer over the image, so the screen looks darker even below the hardware minimum. On AMOLED screens darker pixels use less power, so an overlay can save a little battery. On LCD screens the backlight stays the same, so it does not. For the darkest, most efficient result, put the system slider at minimum first and then add the overlay.

Why the minimum is not lower¶

Phone makers choose the minimum so the screen stays readable in daylight and so the panel behaves well. On some OLED panels colours become uneven or blotchy at very low drive levels. That is part of why the minimum is higher than you might like at night, and why an overlay is the practical way to go further.

PWM flicker¶

Many OLED phones dim by switching pixels on and off very quickly (PWM), often more slowly at low brightness. Some flicker-sensitive people therefore keep the system brightness higher and use an overlay to darken the picture. It is a known workaround, not a guaranteed fix, and how much it helps depends on the phone and the person.

Best practice at night¶

  1. Turn on dark mode, so most apps are mostly black.
  2. Put the system brightness at its minimum (or use Extra dim, if you have it).
  3. Add the dimmer for the last step, and let the schedule do it for you every night.
  4. Turn on Auto-Dim if white pages still catch you out.

Frequently asked questions¶

Does a screen dimmer app save battery?¶

On AMOLED screens, a little, because darker pixels use less power. On LCD screens, hardly at all, because the backlight stays the same. Lowering the system brightness first saves the most.

Is an overlay dimmer bad for an AMOLED screen?¶

No. It only changes what is displayed; darker pixels are, if anything, less wear.

Do screen dimmer overlays show up in screenshots?¶

On most phones, yes. Android screenshots include overlays, so turn dimming off from the notification or tile before taking one.
