Screen dimmer app vs Android's Extra dim¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer

Android's Extra dim (the name on Pixel and Samsung phones; the underlying Android setting is “Reduce Bright Colors”) lowers the screen beyond the normal slider at the system level. A dimmer app such as Screen & Notification Dimmer adds a dark layer on top. Extra dim is built in and covers everything, but it has one fixed strength range and no schedule on many phones. A dimmer app adds finer control, schedules, notification buttons and an emergency off. Using both together gives the darkest screen.

What Extra dim is¶

Google added Extra dim in Android 12. It lives under Settings > Accessibility (sometimes Settings > Display), with an intensity slider and a Quick Settings tile. Samsung has its own "Extra dim" in One UI. Some phone makers don't include it at all, and on others it is hidden. How strong it is depends on the phone.

Use both for the darkest result¶

Extra dim and an overlay stack. Turn Extra dim on at a moderate strength, then use the dimmer for fine control on top. That way you get the darkness of both, plus a schedule and quick buttons.

Does Extra dim save battery?¶

Extra dim usually lowers the actual output of the panel, so it can save a little battery, especially on LCDs. An overlay mostly darkens the image, which on OLED screens also means darker pixels and less power, but on LCD the backlight stays the same. See overlay vs real brightness.

Frequently asked questions¶

Is Android's Extra dim better than a screen dimmer app?¶

Neither is strictly better. Extra dim is built in; a dimmer app adds schedules, timers, notification controls and an emergency off. Together they give the darkest screen.

Why can't I find Extra dim on my phone?¶

It depends on the phone maker and Android version. Search Settings for "Extra dim" or "Reduce bright colours". If it is missing, a dimmer app does the same job.

Can I use Extra dim and a dimmer app together?¶

Yes. They stack, which makes the screen darker than either alone.
