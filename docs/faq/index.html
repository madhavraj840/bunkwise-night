Frequently Asked Questions¶

General¶

What is Night Mode?¶

A screen dimmer for Android by Bunkwise Technologies (Google Play name: Screen Dimmer - Night Mode). It draws a dark layer over your display so you can dim the screen below your phone’s normal minimum brightness. There is no account and there are no ads.

Which Android versions are supported?¶

The minimum supported Android version is shown in the “Requires Android” line on the Google Play listing. If your phone can install the app from Google Play, it is supported.

Is there an iPhone version?¶

No. The phone app is Android only. A separate Night mode for Windows exists for PCs and laptops.

Does dimming with an overlay damage my screen?¶

No. The dimming is a software layer drawn on top of what is on screen. Turning Night Mode off removes the layer straight away.

Is Night Mode an eye-care or medical app?¶

No. It is a screen dimmer that helps you use your phone comfortably in the dark. It makes no medical claims and is not a medical device.

Dimming¶

Can Night Mode really make my screen darker than the lowest brightness setting?¶

Yes. Android’s brightness slider stops at a minimum that can still feel bright in a dark room. Night Mode’s overlay sits on top of that, so it can go further. The app shows the result as a percentage darker than Android’s lowest brightness.

What do the 30%, 45%, 60% and 80% levels mean?¶

They are quick levels for how much darker than Android’s lowest brightness the screen should be. Start with 30% if you are new to the app, then go darker. You can also drag the slider to any value in between.

How is Night Mode different from Android’s built-in Extra dim?¶

Extra dim is a system setting on many phones. Whether you have it, and how strong it is, depends on your phone and Android version. Night Mode is a separate app with an adjustable level, a floating slider over any app, notification buttons and Quick Settings tiles. They can be used side by side, so try both and keep what feels better.

Does Night Mode work with Android dark mode?¶

Yes. Dark mode changes the colours apps use. Night Mode lowers how bright the whole screen looks, including white apps and websites, so the two do different jobs.

Will dimming my screen help me sleep better?¶

We can’t promise that. Lower brightness at night is more comfortable for many people, but Night Mode is not a medical product. Treat it as one small part of a wind-down routine.

Controls¶

How do I change the dimming level without opening the app?¶

Use the buttons in the Night Mode notification (−10, +10, AUTO, OFF), the Night mode and Night mode slider tiles in Quick Settings, or the floating slider that appears over the app you are using.

How do I turn Night Mode off quickly?¶

Tap OFF in the notification, switch off the Quick Settings tile, or shake the phone (on by default). If you enabled the optional Accessibility service, pressing Volume Up and Volume Down together also turns it off.

Permissions and privacy¶

Why does Night Mode need “Display over other apps”?¶

That permission lets the app draw the dark layer over everything on screen. Without it, dimming cannot work.

Is the Accessibility permission required?¶

No, it is optional. The dimmer runs on the overlay permission alone. If you turn Accessibility on, it adds the volume-button emergency shortcut, per-app dimming profiles, and dimming of the notification panel.

Does Night Mode need an account?¶

No. There is no sign-up or login.

What data does Night Mode collect?¶

None. Night Mode does not collect personal data, and everything runs on your device. Purchases are handled by Google Play. See the Privacy Policy.

Are there ads?¶

No. Night Mode has no ads, in the free version or in Pro.

Pro¶

Is Night Mode free?¶

Yes. The core dimmer is free to use. An optional Pro purchase on Google Play adds extra features.

What does Pro include?¶

The edge slider, Auto-Dim, schedules, per-app profiles and colour filters. The User Guide has a full comparison.

Windows¶

Is there a Windows version of Night Mode?¶

Yes. It is a separate, free, open-source tray app for Windows 10 and 11. See Night mode for Windows.
