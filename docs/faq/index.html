Screen & Notification Dimmer Docs › FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions¶

General¶

What is Screen & Notification Dimmer?¶

A screen dimmer for Android by Bunkwise Technologies. It draws a dark layer over your display so you can dim the screen below your phone’s normal minimum brightness. There is no account.

Which Android versions are supported?¶

Android 8.0 (Oreo) and newer. Auto-Dim needs Android 11 or newer. If your phone can install the app from Google Play, it is supported.

Is there an iPhone version?¶

No. The phone app is Android only. A separate Night mode for Windows exists for PCs and laptops.

Does dimming with an overlay damage my screen?¶

No. The dimming is a software layer drawn on top of what is on screen. Turning Screen & Notification Dimmer off removes the layer straight away.

Is Screen & Notification Dimmer an eye-care or medical app?¶

No. It is a screen dimmer that helps you use your phone comfortably in the dark. It makes no medical claims and is not a medical device.

Dimming¶

Can Screen & Notification Dimmer really make my screen darker than the lowest brightness setting?¶

Yes. Android’s brightness slider stops at a minimum that can still feel bright in a dark room. Screen & Notification Dimmer’s overlay sits on top of that, so it can go further. The app shows the result as a percentage darker than Android’s lowest brightness.

What do the 20, 40, 60 and 80 levels mean?¶

They are quick levels for how much darker than Android’s lowest brightness the screen should be. Start with 20 or 40 if you are new to the app, then go darker. You can also drag the slider to any value in between.

How is Screen & Notification Dimmer different from Android’s built-in Extra dim?¶

Extra dim is a system setting on many phones. Whether you have it, and how strong it is, depends on your phone and Android version. Screen & Notification Dimmer is a separate app with an adjustable level, a free schedule, notification buttons, an edge slider (Pro) and Quick Settings tiles. They can be used side by side, so try both and keep what feels better.

Does Screen & Notification Dimmer work with Android dark mode?¶

Yes. Dark mode changes the colours apps use. Screen & Notification Dimmer lowers how bright the whole screen looks, including white apps and websites, so the two do different jobs.

Will dimming my screen help me sleep better?¶

We can’t promise that. Lower brightness at night is more comfortable for many people, but Screen & Notification Dimmer is not a medical product. Treat it as one small part of a wind-down routine.

Controls¶

How do I change the dimming level without opening the app?¶

Use the buttons in the Screen & Notification Dimmer notification (−10, +10, OFF, and AUTO for Auto-Dim), the on/off tile in Quick Settings, or, with Pro, the edge slider and the slider tile.

How do I turn Screen & Notification Dimmer off quickly?¶

Tap OFF in the notification, switch off the Quick Settings tile, If you turned them on in Settings > Emergency escape, shaking the phone or pressing Volume Up and Volume Down together also turns it off. Both are off by default.

Permissions and privacy¶

Why does Screen & Notification Dimmer need “Display over other apps”?¶

That permission lets the app draw the dark layer over everything on screen. Without it, dimming cannot work.

Is the Accessibility permission required?¶

No, it is optional. The dimmer runs on the overlay permission alone. If you turn Accessibility on, it adds dimming of the status bar and notification panel, the volume-button emergency shortcut, per-app levels and Auto-Dim.

Does Screen & Notification Dimmer need an account?¶

No. There is no sign-up or login.

What data does Screen & Notification Dimmer collect?¶

Screen & Notification Dimmer never asks for your name, email or contacts, and your settings stay on your phone. It includes Google AdMob (optional rewarded ads), Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, and Microsoft Clarity, which collect device and usage data such as app screens opened and crash reports, subject to your consent where required. Purchases are handled by Google Play. See the Privacy Policy.

Are there ads?¶

Only if you choose to watch one. Tapping Watch an ad on a locked Pro feature plays a short ad and unlocks that feature for 12 hours. Ads never appear by themselves, and Pro has none.

Pro¶

Is Screen & Notification Dimmer free?¶

Yes. The core dimmer is free to use. The bedtime schedule and timer are free too. Optional Pro, a weekly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase on Google Play (priced by country; see Free vs Pro), adds extra features, and each one can also be unlocked for 12 hours with an ad.

What does Pro include?¶

Dimming the notification bar, status bar and keyboard, the edge slider, Auto-Dim, per-app levels, colour filters, presets and keep-after-restart. The User Guide has a full comparison.

Windows¶

Is there a Windows version of the app?¶

Yes. It is a separate, free, open-source tray app for Windows 10 and 11. See Night mode for Windows.
