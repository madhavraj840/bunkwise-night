A free bedtime schedule that dims the screen for you¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer

Screen & Notification Dimmer can turn dimming on and off by itself every night. Open the Automate tab, switch on Night auto-dim, and choose the times and days. The default is 10:30 PM to 6:00 AM, every day. The schedule is free, with no ads and no trial.

If you have to remember to switch the dimmer on, you will forget on the nights you need it. A schedule fixes that: the screen is already dark when you pick the phone up in bed, and back to normal in the morning.

How to set it up¶

  1. Open the app and go to Automate > Schedule.
  2. Switch on Night auto-dim.
  3. Set the on time and the off time. The app shows how long that is, for example "Dimming for 7h 30m a night, every day".
  4. Under Repeat on, choose every day, weekdays, weekends or any set of days.

The 24-hour strip at the top shows the dimmed window, so overnight times such as 11 PM to 7 AM are easy to check.

How accurate is the timing?¶

The schedule fires within a couple of minutes of the time you set, even when the phone is in Doze. To-the-second timing would need Android's exact alarm permission, and I chose not to ask for it, because a dimmer doesn't need it. If you open the phone exactly at 10:30, give it a minute.

If you set the on and off times to the same value, the app warns you that nothing will happen. If no days are ticked, it says the schedule will never fire.

Making it reliable on Xiaomi, Samsung and others¶

Some phone makers stop background apps more aggressively than stock Android, and that can stop a schedule from firing. Set the app's battery usage to Unrestricted and, on Xiaomi, Vivo and Oppo, allow Autostart. The built-in "Is everything working?" page checks this for you. The guide to dimmers turning off by themselves covers each brand.

Free here, paid elsewhere¶

I checked how other popular dimmers handle this on their Play listings. Night screen lists its "Auto start & stop timer" under premium, and Darker lists "Auto-On & Auto-Off" among its paid features. Penumbra is one that also has a free schedule. Here it is free because it is the feature that matters most for a bedtime dimmer.

Frequently asked questions¶

How do I make my Android screen dim automatically at night?¶

Install Screen & Notification Dimmer, open Automate > Schedule, switch on Night auto-dim and set your times. It is free.

Can I schedule dimming only on weekdays?¶

Yes. Under Repeat on, choose weekdays, weekends, or any individual days.

Why did the schedule start a minute late?¶

Android batches background alarms to save battery, so the schedule fires within a couple of minutes of the set time. The app deliberately does not request the exact-alarm permission.
