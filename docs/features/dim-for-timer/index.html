Dim for a while: a sleep timer for your screen¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer

Tap Dim for in Screen & Notification Dimmer and pick how long: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, until a specific time, a custom length, or until you turn it off. The screen dims at your level, then returns to normal by itself when the time is up. The app shows the time left on the home screen. It is free.

A timer is for the times you know you will only need the dark for a while: reading a few chapters before sleep, a film in a dark room, or a night feed with a baby where you want the phone back to normal afterwards.

Options¶

ChoiceWhat happens
15 min, 30 min, 1 hourDims now, turns off after that long
Until a timeDims until the clock time shown, for example "until 11:45 PM"
Some other lengthType any number of minutes
Until I turn it offPlain dimming with no timer

While a timer runs, the status line says "Back to normal at 11:45 PM" and a countdown shows the time left.

Timer or schedule?¶

Use the bedtime schedule for the same hours every night. Use Dim for for one-off sessions.

Several other dimmers have a timer too. Penumbra offers 15, 30 or 60 minutes from a long-press, for example. The "until a time" option is the one people tend to miss elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions¶

Can a screen dimmer turn off by itself after some time?¶

Yes. Use Dim for in Screen & Notification Dimmer and pick 15, 30 or 60 minutes, a clock time, or a custom length.

Does the timer keep running if I close the app?¶

Yes. It runs in the dimming service, so it works with the app closed or the screen off.
