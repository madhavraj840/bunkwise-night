Dim the notification bar, status bar and keyboard on Android¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

Ordinary overlay dimmers cannot draw over the status bar, the pulled-down notification shade, Quick Settings or the keyboard, so those parts stay bright. Screen & Notification Dimmer can dim them as well. Turn on Dim notification bar in the app and allow its optional Accessibility service; the dark layer then sits above the system areas too. It is a Pro feature, and you can unlock it free for 12 hours by watching one ad.

This is the feature the app is named after. If you have used an overlay dimmer before, you know the moment: the screen is nicely dark, then you pull down the notification shade to check a message and a bright white panel lights up the whole room. In a dark bedroom that is the brightest thing you see all night.

Why most dimmers can't reach the notification shade¶

Android lets apps draw over other apps with the "Display over other apps" permission (its technical name is TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY). For security, Android keeps that kind of window below the system's own surfaces. The status bar, the notification shade, Quick Settings, the keyboard and the lock screen are all drawn above it, and some screens, such as parts of the Settings app, hide ordinary overlays altogether. So an overlay dimmer, however dark it is, has a bright hole exactly where your notifications appear.

There is one window type that sits higher: the Accessibility overlay. Only an app running an Accessibility service may use it. That is the route Screen & Notification Dimmer takes when you switch this feature on.

How to turn it on¶

  1. Open Screen & Notification Dimmer and turn dimming on.
  2. Find Dim notification bar ("Also darkens the status bar and the pulled-down shade") and switch it on. If you do not have Pro, tap Watch an ad to unlock it for 12 hours.
  3. The app explains what the Accessibility service does, then opens Android's Accessibility settings. Go to Downloaded apps (or Installed apps), choose Night mode - better dimming and allow it.
  4. Pull down the shade. It should now be as dark as the rest of the screen.

The notification itself also nudges you: if the service is off, it shows "Tap here: allow Accessibility to dim this panel too".

Some phones add a step

On Android 13 and newer, an app installed from outside Google Play may show "Restricted setting" when you try to allow Accessibility. Installed from Play, you normally won't see this. On Xiaomi, Vivo and Oppo phones the Accessibility list can be under "Downloaded apps" or "Installed services".

What the Accessibility service can and cannot see¶

Accessibility is a powerful permission, so it is fair to ask what it is used for. In this app the service draws the dark layer over system areas. If you switch the related features on, it also watches for the Volume Up + Volume Down emergency combination, notes which app is in front for per-app levels, and, for Auto-Dim, measures how bright the screen is from a tiny downscaled screenshot that is thrown away straight after. It does not read the text on your screen, record what you type, or control other apps. Each of those extras has its own switch. The full details are in the privacy policy, section 1.

How it compares with other apps¶

A few dimmers offer something similar, usually as a paid extra. According to their Google Play listings, Night screen calls it "Advanced dimming" in its premium tier, Super Dim extends its filter over system areas in premium, and Darker sells "Darken navigation bar" as a paid feature. Plain overlay apps such as Dimmer: Night Screen Overlay do not mention covering the status bar at all. What is different here is that you can try it free for 12 hours with one ad before deciding whether it is worth paying for.

Frequently asked questions¶

Why is my notification panel still bright when the dimmer is on?¶

Because ordinary overlays are drawn below the notification shade. To dim it, an app needs an Accessibility overlay. In Screen & Notification Dimmer, turn on Dim notification bar and allow the "Night mode - better dimming" Accessibility service.

Can I dim the keyboard on Android?¶

Yes. With Dim notification bar on and the Accessibility service allowed, the keyboard is dimmed along with the rest of the screen.

Is Dim notification bar free?¶

It is a Pro feature. You can unlock it for 12 hours by watching one short ad, as many times as you like, or buy Pro to keep it on permanently.

Does it work on Samsung, Xiaomi and OnePlus?¶

Yes, it uses a standard Android window type. The only difference between brands is where the Accessibility switch sits in Settings.
