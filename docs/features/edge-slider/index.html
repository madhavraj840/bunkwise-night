Edge slider: change the darkness by sliding along the screen edge¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

The edge slider lets you change how dark the screen is without opening Screen & Notification Dimmer. Slide a finger down the right (or left) edge of the screen to make it darker, or up to make it brighter, inside any app. A volume-style bar shows the level, then hides itself. Tap the moon icon on the bar to switch dimming off. It is a Pro feature, and one ad unlocks it for 12 hours.

Dimming is not a set-and-forget thing. A dark film scene needs less, a white web page needs more, and you only find out once you are already in the app. Going back to the dimmer every time breaks what you were doing, so I wanted the control to be where your thumb already is, the same way the volume bar appears when you press a button.

How the edge slider works¶

  • Slide down along the edge for darker, slide up for brighter.
  • The bar appears at the edge with the current level, follows your finger, and hides itself after a moment. A tap anywhere else hides it at once.
  • Tap the moon icon at the foot of the bar to turn dimming off.
  • AUTO under the bar switches on Auto-Dim.
  • Choose the right edge or the left edge. Left-handed users usually prefer the left.
  • The bar and its handle are kept above the dark layer, so they are never dimmed themselves and you can always see them.

The Night slider Quick Settings tile opens the same bar. That helps if you would rather start from the pull-down panel than from the edge.

How to set it up¶

  1. Open Screen & Notification Dimmer and find Edge slider on the Dim screen.
  2. Switch it on. Without Pro, tap Watch an ad to unlock it for 12 hours.
  3. Pick Right edge or Left edge under Settings > Control from anywhere.
  4. Open any app and slide along that edge.

Will it get in the way of the back gesture?¶

Android's back gesture is a sideways swipe in from the edge, while the edge slider follows a movement along the edge, up or down. The handle is a thin strip, so an ordinary back swipe still works. If an app of yours relies heavily on edge swipes (some drawing and reading apps do), move the slider to the other side.

Is this common in other dimmers?¶

Not that I found. I read the Google Play listings of the dimmers that come up most often in searches (Penumbra, Night screen, Darker, Super Dim and others). They offer sliders inside the app, in the notification or in a tile, but none of them describes an edge gesture that works inside other apps. If that changes, I will update this page.

Frequently asked questions¶

How do I change screen dimming without opening the app?¶

Use the edge slider (slide along the screen edge), the −10 and +10 buttons on the notification, or the Night slider tile in Quick Settings.

Can I put the edge slider on the left side?¶

Yes. Choose Left edge in the app's settings.

Does the edge slider turn dimming on?¶

Sliding only changes the level. Dragging it all the way up makes the layer clear but does not switch the service off. To turn dimming off, tap the moon icon on the bar.
