Emergency off: getting your screen back when it is too dark¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

If the screen is too dark to see, you can turn Screen & Notification Dimmer off without looking. Shake the phone, or press Volume Up and Volume Down together. Both are off by default and switched on in Settings > Emergency escape. The OFF button on the notification and the Quick Settings tile also always work. The dark layer is also capped, so the screen never goes completely black.

Every dimmer has the same nightmare case. You drag the slider too far, the screen goes nearly black, and now you can't see the button that would fix it. I wanted at least one way out that needs no eyesight at all.

Every way to switch it off¶

When an escape fires, the app tells you what happened ("You pressed Volume Up + Down together") so it never looks like a crash.

Why both are off by default¶

Shake used to be on from the start. The trouble is that a phone carried in a pocket or bag gets shaken too, and the dimming would vanish for no visible reason. Pressing both volume keys also happens by accident, for example when you grab the phone in bed. A dimmer that switches itself off unasked feels broken, so both escapes are now opt-in. Turn on whichever suits you the first time you set the app up.

Adjusting the shake¶

Shake has a strength setting. If it fires while you walk, make it firmer; if it never fires, make it lighter. The volume combination is more precise and works inside any app, so it is the better choice if you can allow the Accessibility service.

A safety cap on darkness¶

Even at 100 the dark layer is not fully opaque. It stops at 97%, so on most phones you can still make out the screen in a dark room. That is deliberate. A fully black overlay would look exactly like a phone that has switched off.

Is this unusual?¶

Shake-to-disable is fairly common. Penumbra and Super Dim both list it. The volume-button combination is less common. I didn't find it in the listings of the other dimmers I compared, and it is the one I would pick, because it works without any guesswork.

Frequently asked questions¶

My screen is too dark to see. How do I turn the dimmer off?¶

Press Volume Up and Volume Down together, or shake the phone, if you enabled those. Otherwise pull down from the top of the screen and tap OFF on the dimmer notification or tap the on/off tile. Restarting the phone also clears the overlay, unless keep-after-restart is on.

Is shake to turn off on by default?¶

No. In current versions both shake and the volume-button escape are off until you switch them on under Settings > Emergency escape.

Can the screen go completely black?¶

No. The dark layer is capped at 97% opacity, so the screen stays faintly visible.
