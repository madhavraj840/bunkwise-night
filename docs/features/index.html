Everything Screen & Notification Dimmer can do¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

Screen & Notification Dimmer makes an Android screen darker than its lowest brightness setting. The free app covers the essentials: dimming to any level, a bedtime schedule, a timer, notification buttons, a Quick Settings tile and an emergency off. Pro adds the things most overlay dimmers cannot do: dimming the status bar and notification shade, an edge slider, Auto-Dim and a level for each app. You can unlock any Pro feature free for 12 hours by watching one ad.

I started this app because my own phone was still too bright at night with the brightness slider at zero. Most of the features below came from the same kind of small annoyance: the notification shade lighting up the room, a white web page flashing me at 1 AM, or reaching for the app in the dark and not finding it. Each page linked here explains one feature in detail, including its limits.

What the free version includes¶

The free version is a complete dimmer, not a trial. It has no time limit, and nothing stops working after a few days. You get the full 0 to 100 range, the quick levels (20, 40, 60 and 80), the bedtime schedule, the timer, the notification buttons, the on/off tile, usage statistics and the "Is everything working?" checker, which finds the battery settings that make some phones kill background apps.

I made the bedtime schedule free on purpose. Most dimmers I looked at put scheduling behind a payment, and it is the one feature that means you never have to open the app at night.

What Pro adds¶

Pro is for the parts of the screen and the situations an ordinary overlay misses. A standard "Display over other apps" layer cannot cover the status bar, the pulled-down notification shade or the keyboard, so those stay bright. With Pro and the optional Accessibility service, the dark layer covers them too. Pro also adds the edge slider, Auto-Dim, per-app levels, colour filters, presets and keep-after-restart.

Using Pro without paying¶

If you only need a Pro feature now and then, you do not have to buy anything. Tap Watch an ad on a locked feature, and that one feature is unlocked for 12 hours. Watch three ads within 12 hours and all of Pro unlocks for 12 hours. Ads only play when you tap that button yourself. They never appear while you are dimming or reading. The Free vs Pro guide explains it step by step.

Frequently asked questions¶

Is Screen & Notification Dimmer free?¶

Yes. Dimming below minimum brightness, the bedtime schedule, the timer, the notification buttons, the tile and emergency off are free with no time limit. Pro features can be bought, or unlocked for 12 hours by watching a short ad.

Which Android versions does it support?¶

Android 8.0 (Oreo) and newer. Auto-Dim needs Android 11 or newer, because it uses a screenshot API that older versions do not have.

Does it need root?¶

No. It uses the standard "Display over other apps" permission, plus the optional Accessibility service for the status bar and other system areas.
