Control dimming from the notification and Quick Settings¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer

While dimming is on, Screen & Notification Dimmer shows a notification with four buttons: −10 (lighter), +10 (darker), AUTO (Auto-Dim) and OFF. Pull down the shade and tap; you never need to open the app. There is also an on/off tile for Quick Settings. The notification buttons and the on/off tile are free.

Most of the time you want to change dimming while doing something else, such as reading or watching. The notification is the one place that is always a swipe away, so the controls belong there.

The notification buttons¶

ButtonWhat it doesFree?
−10Makes the screen 10 points lighterFree
+10Makes the screen 10 points darkerFree
AUTOTurns Auto-Dim on or offPro, or 12 h with an ad
OFFSwitches dimming off at onceFree

The title shows the level, for example "Dimming on - 40%". You can hide the buttons in the app's settings if you would rather have a plain status notification.

Keep notifications allowed

On Android 13 and newer, apps must ask before showing notifications. If you tapped "Don't allow", the buttons can't appear. Turn notifications back on in the app ("Turn on notifications to dim from the shade") or in Android Settings > Apps &gt; Screen & Notification Dimmer > Notifications. The notification is also the most reliable way to switch dimming off, so it is worth keeping.

Quick Settings tiles¶

  • Screen & Notification Dimmer toggles dimming at your current level and shows it, for example "On · 40%". Free.
  • Screen & Notification Dimmer slider opens the volume-style dimming bar over whatever you are doing, the same bar as the edge slider. Pro, or 12 hours with an ad.

To add them: swipe down twice from the top of the screen, tap the edit pencil, then drag "Screen & Notification Dimmer" and "Screen & Notification Dimmer slider" up into your active tiles. On Samsung the edit button sits behind the three-dot menu, and on Xiaomi HyperOS it is the edit icon in the Control Centre.

Will the notification bother me?¶

It only shows while dimming is on, because Android needs a visible notification to keep the dimming service alive. Other notifications from the app are rare and never break through Do Not Disturb.

The panel you pull down to reach these buttons is bright by default. If that bothers you, see how to dim the notification bar itself.

Frequently asked questions¶

Can I adjust dimming without opening the app?¶

Yes. Use the −10 and +10 buttons on the notification, the on/off tile in Quick Settings, or the edge slider.

Why did the dimmer notification disappear?¶

It only shows while dimming is on. If dimming is on and there is no notification, notifications for the app are probably blocked. Allow them in Android Settings > Apps &gt; Screen & Notification Dimmer > Notifications.

Is there a screen dimmer Quick Settings tile?¶

Yes. Screen & Notification Dimmer adds an on/off tile (free) and a slider tile (Pro) to Quick Settings.
