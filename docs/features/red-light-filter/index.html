Red, amber and custom colour filters¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer

Screen & Notification Dimmer can tint its dark layer instead of using plain black. Pick Red night light, Amber or a custom colour, and the screen becomes darker and warmer in one step. Black is free. The tints are part of Pro, and one ad unlocks them for 12 hours. Custom colours are automatically kept dark enough to read.

Some people want less light, and some want warmer light. Many want both at bedtime. Android's Night Light warms the colours but cannot go below minimum brightness, and most blue-light filter apps are the other way round. The filter here adds the colour to the dimming layer itself, so you get both from one control.

The filters¶

FilterWhat it looks likeGood for
BlackJust darker, colours unchangedFilms, photos, anything where colour matters. Free
Red night lightDeep red tintVery dark rooms, astronomy, keeping the room as dark as possible
AmberWarm, like a bedside lampReading and scrolling in bed
Custom colourAny tint you pick, auto-darkened to stay readableMatching your own taste

Filter or Android's Night Light?¶

You can use both. Night Light changes the colour temperature at the system level. This filter adds a tinted dark layer on top. Together they give a warmer image than either alone. If your goal is mainly warmth with only a little dimming, a dedicated filter such as Twilight may suit you better. If your goal is darkness first, a dimmer with a tint is the better fit. The dimmer vs blue light filter page goes through the difference.

A note on claims: I am not going to tell you a red filter improves your sleep or protects your eyes. This is a screen dimmer, not a medical device. Plenty of people simply find warm, dim light more comfortable at night, and that is what the filter is for.

Frequently asked questions¶

Is there a red screen filter app for Android?¶

Yes. Screen & Notification Dimmer has a Red night light filter that tints the dimming layer red, alongside Amber and custom colours.

Does the colour filter work below minimum brightness?¶

Yes. The tint is part of the dimming layer, so the screen gets darker and warmer at the same time.
