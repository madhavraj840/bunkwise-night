Free vs Pro, and unlocking Pro without paying¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

Screen & Notification Dimmer is free to use with no time limit: dimming below minimum brightness, the bedtime schedule, the timer, the notification buttons, the on/off tile and emergency off. Pro adds notification-bar dimming, the edge slider, Auto-Dim, per-app levels, colour filters, presets and keep-after-restart. You can buy Pro, or watch one short ad to unlock any single Pro feature for 12 hours. Watch three ads within 12 hours and all of Pro unlocks for 12 hours.

How the ad unlock works¶

  1. Tap a locked Pro switch. A sheet opens with Watch an ad at the top.
  2. Watch the short ad to the end.
  3. That feature is unlocked for 12 hours. The switch shows "Unlocked until 9:40 AM".
  4. Each ad also counts towards all of Pro: three ads within 12 hours unlock every Pro feature for 12 hours from the last one.

Ads only play when you tap that button yourself. There are no banner ads and no ads that pop up while you dim, read or watch. If you are in the EEA or UK, Google's consent form asks about ad personalisation first, and you can change your choice later in Settings.

Buying Pro: weekly or lifetime¶

If you would rather not watch ads, Pro is sold through Google Play in two ways:

  • Weekly subscription. It renews every week until you cancel, which you can do any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. First-time subscribers may be offered a cheaper first week.
  • Lifetime. One payment, and Pro stays unlocked for good.

Prices are set country by country, so they are lower in some places than others. The Pro page in the app always shows the exact price in your currency before you pay. These are the Google Play prices as of 25 November 2026:

Purchases are tied to your Google account, so Pro comes back if you reinstall on the same account. There is no separate login. If you subscribed earlier on a monthly or six-month plan, you keep Pro for as long as that subscription runs.

Why it works this way¶

A dimmer is a small app, and I didn't want the basic job to be a trial that expires. The free version had to be good enough to use every night, schedule included. Pro pays for the harder parts, such as the Accessibility overlay and Auto-Dim, which need a lot of testing across phone brands. The ad unlock is there so nobody has to pay just to try them.

Privacy¶

There is no account. Dimming works offline. The app includes Google AdMob for the optional ads, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for anonymous usage and crash reports, and Microsoft Clarity. All of them are described in the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions¶

Can I use Screen & Notification Dimmer for free?¶

Yes. The dimmer, schedule, timer, notification buttons, tile and emergency off are free forever. Pro features can be unlocked for 12 hours with an ad.

How long does an ad unlock last?¶

12 hours. One ad unlocks one feature; three ads within 12 hours unlock all of Pro for 12 hours.

Does the app show ads while I am dimming?¶

No. Ads only play when you tap Watch an ad to unlock a Pro feature.

How much does Pro cost?¶

Pro is a weekly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase on Google Play, priced by country. The weekly plan is ₹25 in India, US$0.49 in the United States, £0.49 in the UK and €0.49 to €0.59 in the euro area. Lifetime is ₹379, US$9.99, £8.99 and €8.79 to €10.99. The app shows your exact local price before you pay.

Is there a lifetime option?¶

Yes. Lifetime is a single payment that unlocks Pro for good. It is ₹379 in India, US$9.99 in the United States, £8.99 in the UK and €8.79 to €10.99 in the euro area, and the Pro page shows your local price.
