Phone still too bright at its lowest brightness? 7 fixes¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

Phone makers set a minimum brightness that is meant for daylight, so in a dark room it can still feel harsh. Work through these in order: turn off adaptive brightness and drag the slider to zero, turn on dark mode, turn on Extra dim if your phone has it, add Night Light for warmth, then use a screen dimmer app such as Screen & Notification Dimmer to go below the minimum. A dimmer that also covers the notification shade fixes the last bright spot.

1. Drag the brightness slider fully left¶

Pull down Quick Settings and move brightness to the very bottom. If adaptive (auto) brightness is on, the phone may raise it again a moment later. Turn it off for the night, or simply drag it down once more, since adaptive brightness learns from your corrections.

2. Turn on dark mode¶

Settings > Display > Dark theme. Most of the glare at night comes from white backgrounds, and dark mode removes a lot of them. On AMOLED screens it also saves battery.

3. Use Extra dim if your phone has it¶

Search Settings for "Extra dim" or "Reduce bright colours". It lowers the screen past the normal minimum at the system level, and you can add its Quick Settings tile. Not every phone has it. See Extra dim vs a dimmer app.

4. Add Night Light for warmth¶

Night Light (or Eye Comfort Shield, Reading mode and similar, depending on the brand) doesn't make the screen darker, but a warmer screen feels less harsh. Schedule it for sunset to sunrise.

5. Use a screen dimmer app to go below the minimum¶

This is the step that actually breaks through the floor. A dimmer app draws a dark layer over everything. In Screen & Notification Dimmer:

  1. Install it from Google Play and allow "Display over other apps".
  2. Tap a quick level: 20, 40, 60 or 80. Start at 40, then go darker.
  3. Turn on the free bedtime schedule so it happens every night by itself.

6. Fix the bright notification shade¶

Once everything else is dark, the pulled-down shade becomes the brightest thing on the phone. Turn on Dim notification bar so the overlay covers the status bar, the shade and the keyboard too.

7. Stop sudden white pages¶

If a web page or video still flashes you, turn on Auto-Dim. It darkens the layer when the content turns bright.

Set up an escape first

Before you go very dark, turn on shake or Volume Up + Volume Down under Settings > Emergency escape. Then you can always get the screen back without seeing it. How emergency off works.

Frequently asked questions¶

Why is my phone so bright at night even on the lowest setting?¶

The minimum brightness is set so the screen stays readable in daylight. In a dark room that floor can still be too bright, which is why dimmer apps exist.

How do I make my screen darker than the lowest brightness on Android?¶

Use Extra dim if your phone has it, and/or a screen dimmer app such as Screen & Notification Dimmer, which draws a dark layer over the screen.

Is it safe to make the screen that dark?¶

Yes. Screen & Notification Dimmer caps the layer at 97% so the screen never goes fully black, and you can turn it off by shaking the phone or pressing both volume buttons if you enable those.
