Why your screen dimmer turns off by itself, and how to fix it¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

A dimmer that switches off on its own is almost always the phone's battery manager closing it in the background. Fix it by setting the app's battery usage to Unrestricted, allowing Autostart on Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo and Realme, and, on Samsung, keeping it out of Sleeping apps. Screen & Notification Dimmer has an "Is everything working?" page that checks these for you and links to the right screen.

Stock Android lets a foreground service with a visible notification keep running, and that is how a dimmer stays on. Several phone makers add their own, stricter battery managers on top, and those can stop the service anyway, especially overnight. The website dontkillmyapp.com tracks this brand by brand.

First, use the built-in checker¶

Open Screen & Notification Dimmer and go to Settings > Is everything working?. It checks the overlay permission, notifications, the Accessibility service, battery optimisation and Battery Saver, and counts how often the service stopped unexpectedly this week. On brands known for strict battery managers it shows a tip such as "Xiaomi phones stop background apps harder than stock Android", with buttons for Allow autostart and Set battery to unrestricted.

Settings by brand¶

BrandWhat to change
Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO (HyperOS / MIUI)Settings > Apps > Manage apps > Night mode: Autostart on; Battery saver: No restrictions; Other permissions: "Display pop-up windows while running in the background" allowed. Lock the app in Recents (pull its card down)
Samsung (One UI)Settings > Apps > Night mode > Battery: Unrestricted. Settings > Battery > Background usage limits: make sure it is not in Sleeping or Deep sleeping apps
OnePlus (OxygenOS)Settings > Apps > Night mode > Battery usage: allow background activity / Unrestricted
Vivo, iQOO (Funtouch / OriginOS)Settings > Battery > Background power consumption management: allow high background usage; Autostart on in i Manager
Oppo, Realme (ColorOS / Realme UI)Settings > Apps > Night mode > Battery usage: allow background activity and auto launch
Pixel and stock AndroidSettings > Apps > Night mode > App battery usage: Unrestricted

Menu names change between versions, so use the Settings search if a path doesn't match exactly.

Other causes¶

  • Battery Saver on: some phones close background apps harder while it is active.
  • Emergency off triggered: if you enabled shake, a shake in a pocket switches dimming off. The app shows "Night mode turned off by emergency escape" when that happens. Make the shake firmer, or use the volume buttons instead.
  • Restarted phone: dimming doesn't come back after a reboot unless keep-after-restart (Pro) is on. The schedule does re-arm itself after a restart.
  • A timer ran out: if you used Dim for, it turns off on purpose.

If the app does stop unexpectedly, it shows "Dimming stopped. Tap to restore." when you next open it.

Frequently asked questions¶

Why does my screen dimmer keep turning off?¶

Usually the phone's battery manager closes it. Set the app's battery usage to Unrestricted and, on Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo and Realme, allow Autostart.

Why does my dimmer stop on Xiaomi?¶

HyperOS and MIUI stop background apps aggressively. Allow Autostart, set Battery saver to No restrictions, and allow pop-up windows in Other permissions.

Does the dimming schedule survive a restart?¶

Yes. The schedule re-arms itself after the phone boots. Restoring the current dimming after a restart needs keep-after-restart (Pro).
