Using your phone in bed without waking anyone¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer

To use your phone next to a sleeping partner or baby, take away the light first and the sound second. Dim the screen below its minimum with a dimmer such as Screen & Notification Dimmer (40 to 80 is usually enough), make sure the notification shade is dimmed too, turn the screen away from them, and switch on Do Not Disturb. A bedtime schedule makes all of this automatic.

Light is what wakes people. A phone at minimum brightness can still throw a surprising glow onto a white ceiling or a pillow, especially when a white web page opens. Here is the setup I recommend, in the order that makes the most difference.

The setup¶

  1. Dark mode on, so most apps are mostly black.
  2. Brightness at minimum, adaptive brightness off.
  3. Dimmer at 40–80. In Screen & Notification Dimmer, the free bedtime schedule switches it on at 10:30 PM by default.
  4. Dim the notification shade, the single brightest thing left. How.
  5. Auto-Dim on if you browse, so white pages don't light up the room. How.
  6. Do Not Disturb and vibration off, so nothing buzzes on the bedside table.
  7. Tilt the screen away from them, and hold the phone lower, closer to your chest.

Night feeds and checking on a baby¶

For a night feed, use Dim for with 30 or 60 minutes, so the phone goes back to normal afterwards without you thinking about it. The red night light filter keeps the room as dark as possible if you need to glance at the screen often.

A table of starting levels¶

SituationStarting level
Reading in bed, lamp off40
Partner asleep beside you60
Baby in the room60–80, red filter
Very dark room, OLED phone80

Every panel is different, so treat these as starting points and adjust with the notification's −10 and +10 buttons.

Frequently asked questions¶

How do I stop my phone screen waking my partner?¶

Dim it below minimum brightness with a dimmer app, also dim the notification shade, use dark mode, and turn the screen away from them.

What dimming level should I use in bed?¶

Start at 40 to 60 and adjust. In a very dark room, 80 is common on OLED phones.
