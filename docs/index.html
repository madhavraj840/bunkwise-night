What is Night Mode?

Night Mode (Screen Dimmer - Night Mode) is a free Android app that makes your screen darker than the lowest brightness Android allows. It works as an overlay: a dark layer drawn over everything on screen, so any app, website or video gets dimmer.

It is built for reading in bed, using a phone next to someone who is sleeping, or checking a notification in a dark room without being hit by light. You choose how dark, and the app shows it as a percentage darker than Android’s own minimum.

How do I make my Android screen darker than the lowest brightness?

It takes about two minutes. The steps below are also in the full guide.

STEP 1

Install the app

Get Night Mode on Google Play and open it.

STEP 2

Allow “Display over other apps”

This permission lets Night Mode draw the dark layer. No account or sign-up is needed.

STEP 3

Pick how dark

Tap 30%, 45%, 60% or 80%, or drag the slider. Shake the phone any time to turn it off.

Frequently asked questions

What is Night Mode (Screen Dimmer - Night Mode)?

Night Mode is a free Android screen dimmer by Bunkwise Technologies. It draws a dark layer over the screen so the display can go darker than the lowest brightness Android allows. It has no account and no ads. Get it on Google Play.

Can an app make my Android screen darker than the minimum brightness?

Yes. Android’s brightness slider stops at a minimum that can still feel bright in a dark room. Night Mode adds an overlay on top of that, so you can go further. The app shows the result as a percentage darker than Android’s lowest brightness, with quick levels of 30%, 45%, 60% and 80%.

How is Night Mode different from Android’s built-in Extra dim?

Extra dim is a built-in setting on many phones, but it depends on your phone and Android version, and its strength is usually fixed. Night Mode works as a separate app with an adjustable level, a floating slider you can use over any app, notification buttons and Quick Settings tiles. Pro adds schedules, Auto-Dim and per-app profiles.

Is Night Mode free? Are there ads?

The dimmer is free to use and has no ads. An optional Pro purchase on Google Play adds extra features (see “What does Pro include?”).

What if the screen becomes too dark to see?

Shake the phone (on by default), or tap Off in the Night Mode notification, or use the Quick Settings tile. If you enabled the optional Accessibility service, pressing Volume Up and Volume Down together also turns it off. Starting at a lighter preset such as 30% is a good way to learn the levels.

Night Mode is not dimming my screen. What should I check?
  • Make sure “Display over other apps” is allowed for Night Mode in your phone’s settings.
  • Check that Night Mode is switched on and the level is above 0%.
  • If Android says “Screen overlay detected” while you grant a permission, close other apps that draw over the screen (chat bubbles, other dimmers or filters) and try again.
  • Open the app once after a restart, and keep its notification enabled so Android does not stop it.
