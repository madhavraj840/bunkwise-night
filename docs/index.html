Night Mode: Screen Dimmer for Android¶

Welcome to the official documentation for Night Mode (Google Play name: Screen Dimmer - Night Mode), a screen dimmer for Android that makes your display darker than your phone’s lowest brightness setting. It is made by Bunkwise Technologies, India.

Get it on Google Play

What is Night Mode?¶

Night Mode draws a dark layer over your entire display so you can dim the screen below your phone’s normal minimum brightness. You choose how dark, and the app shows it as a percentage darker than Android’s lowest brightness. It is built for reading in bed, using a phone next to someone who is asleep, or checking a notification in a dark room without being hit by light.

Key features¶

  • Dims below minimum brightness - useful in a dark room where the lowest system setting still feels too bright
  • Quick levels - 30%, 45%, 60% and 80% darker than Android’s lowest brightness, or drag the slider to any value
  • Floating in-app control - a brightness slider with an AUTO button that sits over whatever app you are using
  • Notification controls - −10, +10, AUTO and OFF buttons right in the notification panel
  • Quick Settings tiles - a Night mode on/off tile and a Night mode slider tile
  • Emergency off - shake the phone, or press Volume Up and Volume Down together if you enabled the optional Accessibility service
  • Private - no account, no ads and no data collection; everything runs on your device
  • Optional Pro - edge slider, Auto-Dim, schedules, per-app profiles and colour filters

Quick start¶

  1. Install Night Mode from Google Play and open it
  2. Grant “Display over other apps” when asked - this is what lets Night Mode draw its dark layer
  3. Pick a level - tap 30%, 45%, 60% or 80%, or drag the slider
  4. Adjust from anywhere - use the floating slider, the notification buttons or the Quick Settings tiles
  5. Turn it off any time from the app, the notification, the tile, or by shaking the phone

Requirements¶

  • An Android phone with Google Play (see “Requires Android” on the listing for the minimum version)
  • Display over other apps permission - required, it draws the dimming layer
  • Accessibility service - optional, only for the volume-button emergency shortcut, per-app profiles and dimming the notification panel

See the User Guide for what each permission is used for.
