Night Mode: Screen Dimmer for Android¶

Welcome to the official documentation for Night Mode (Google Play name: Screen & Notification Dimmer), a screen dimmer for Android that makes your display darker than your phone’s lowest brightness setting, including the notification shade and status bar. It was previously listed as Notification Screen Dimmer. It is made by Bunkwise Technologies, India, and the current version is 8.0.2 (21 November 2026).

Get it on Google Play

What is Night Mode?¶

Night Mode draws a dark layer over your display so you can dim the screen below your phone’s normal minimum brightness. You choose how dark, and the app shows it as a percentage darker than Android’s lowest brightness. It is built for reading in bed, using a phone next to someone who is asleep, or checking a notification in a dark room without being hit by light.

Key features¶

  • Dims below minimum brightness - useful in a dark room where the lowest system setting still feels too bright
  • Quick levels - 20, 40, 60 and 80 (or your own presets), or drag the slider to any value
  • Free bedtime schedule - on at 10:30 PM and off at 6 AM by default; change the times and days
  • Dim for - dim for 15, 30 or 60 minutes or until a set time, then back to normal by itself
  • Dims the notification bar too - with the optional Accessibility service, also darkens the status bar, notification shade, Quick Settings and keyboard (Pro)
  • Edge slider - slide along the screen edge to dim or brighten inside any app (Pro)
  • Notification controls - −10, +10 and OFF buttons right in the notification panel (AUTO turns on Auto-Dim)
  • Quick Settings tiles - a Night mode on/off tile, plus a Night slider tile in Pro
  • Emergency off - shake the phone, or press Volume Up and Volume Down together. Both are off until you turn them on in Settings > Emergency escape
  • No account - no sign-up, and dimming works offline. Ads appear only if you choose to watch one to unlock a Pro feature
  • Optional Pro - notification-bar dimming, edge slider, Auto-Dim, per-app levels, colour filters, presets and keep-after-restart. One short ad unlocks any Pro feature for 12 hours

Quick start¶

  1. Install Night Mode from Google Play and open it
  2. Grant “Display over other apps” when asked - this is what lets Night Mode draw its dark layer
  3. Pick a level - tap 20, 40, 60 or 80, or drag the slider
  4. Set the schedule - turn on the free bedtime schedule under Automate so it dims by itself every night
  5. Adjust from anywhere - use the notification buttons, the Quick Settings tile, or the Pro edge slider
  6. Turn it off any time from the app, the notification or the tile. Turn on shake or Volume Up + Down under Settings > Emergency escape for a blind way out

Requirements¶

  • An Android phone running Android 8.0 or newer, with Google Play. Auto-Dim needs Android 11 or newer
  • Display over other apps permission - required, it draws the dimming layer
  • Notifications - for the control notification with its buttons
  • Accessibility service - optional, only for dimming the status bar and notification shade, the volume-button emergency shortcut, per-app levels and Auto-Dim

See the User Guide for what each permission is used for.
