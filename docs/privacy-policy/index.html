Privacy Policy¶

How Night Mode handles your data.

Privacy at a glance¶

  • No account. There is no sign-up or login
  • Ads only if you choose. A short rewarded ad plays only when you tap Watch an ad to unlock a Pro feature. Pro has no ads
  • No personal details asked for. Your settings stay on your phone and dimming works offline. The app includes Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, and Microsoft Clarity, which collect device and usage data (for example screens opened and crash reports), subject to your consent where required
  • Payments for optional Pro are handled by Google Play
  • Permissions: “Display over other apps” is required to draw the dimming layer. Accessibility is optional and only adds dimming of the status bar and notification panel, the volume-button shortcut, per-app levels and Auto-Dim

Full policy¶

The complete, legally binding Night Mode privacy policy is published at bunkwise.in/privacypolicynightmode.html. If anything on this page differs from that policy, the full policy applies.

Windows app¶

Night mode for Windows works fully offline, makes no network connections and has no account, ads or analytics. Settings stay on your PC.

This website¶

This documentation site may use analytics to count visits. You can change your choice any time with Cookie settings in the page footer. See the website privacy page.

Questions about privacy? Email support@bunkwise.in.
