Terms for using Screen & Notification Dimmer.

Using Screen & Notification Dimmer¶

Screen & Notification Dimmer is provided by Bunkwise Technologies. By installing or using the app you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

Free use and Pro¶

The core dimmer is free. Optional Pro features are sold through Google Play and are subject to Google Play’s payment, refund and subscription terms. Purchases are tied to your Google account.

Safe use¶

  • Screen & Notification Dimmer makes your screen darker. Do not use it in situations where you need to see the screen clearly, such as while driving
  • If the screen becomes too dark, use Emergency off: shake the phone, tap OFF in the notification, or switch off the Quick Settings tile
  • Screen & Notification Dimmer is a screen dimmer. It is not a medical device and makes no medical claims

Permissions¶

Screen & Notification Dimmer needs Display over other apps to work. The Accessibility service is optional and is used only for the features described in the User Guide. You can revoke either permission at any time in Android settings.

No warranty¶

The app is provided “as is”, without warranties of any kind. Dimming appearance depends on your phone, its screen and its Android version, so results can vary.
