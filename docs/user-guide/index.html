User Guide¶

Everything Night Mode can do, and how to set it up.

Getting started¶

  1. Install Night Mode from Google Play and open it
  2. Turn on Display over other apps when asked - Night Mode needs this to draw its dark layer over your screen
  3. Back on the home screen, pick a level or drag the slider to start dimming

No account, sign-up or login is needed.

The home screen¶

The home screen shows everything at a glance:

  • Status line - for example “Off · Android’s own minimum” when dimming is off
  • Large percentage - how much darker than Android’s lowest brightness the screen is
  • Quick levels - 30%, 45%, 60% and 80%, with a tick on the active one
  • Vertical slider - slide towards Darker or Lighter to fine-tune the level
  • Help icon - top right, for help inside the app

Choosing a level¶

Level When to use it
30% A gentle start. Good for the first night or a dim lamp-lit room
45% Reading in bed with the lights off
60% A dark room where even the lowest brightness feels harsh
80% Very dark rooms, or sharing a bed with someone who is asleep

These are suggestions. Every phone panel is different, so move the slider until the screen feels right.

Floating in-app control¶

A floating control sits over the app you are using, so you can adjust dimming without leaving it. It has:

  • a vertical slider with the current level at the top
  • a ring button to switch dimming on or off
  • an AUTO button to hand control to automatic mode

The notification¶

Night Mode shows a notification with the current level and four buttons:

Button What it does
−10 Makes the screen 10 points lighter
+10 Makes the screen 10 points darker
AUTO Switches to automatic mode
OFF Turns dimming off straight away

If the notification shows “Tap here: allow Accessibility to dim this panel”, tap it to enable the optional Accessibility service. That lets Night Mode dim the notification panel itself.

Quick Settings tiles¶

Night Mode adds two tiles to your Quick Settings panel:

  • Night mode - one tap to turn dimming on or off, and it shows its current state
  • Night mode slider - a brightness slider you can drag from the panel

To add them, swipe down twice from the top of the screen, tap the edit (pencil) icon, then drag the Night Mode tiles into your active tiles. The exact steps vary a little between phone makers.

Emergency off¶

If the screen ever gets too dark to see, use any of these:

  1. Shake the phone - on by default
  2. Tap OFF in the Night Mode notification
  3. Switch off the Quick Settings tile
  4. Press Volume Up and Volume Down together - needs the optional Accessibility service

Learn the levels first

Start at 30% and work upwards. You will quickly learn how dark each level is on your phone.

Permissions¶

Permission Required? What it is used for
Display over other apps Required Draws the dark layer over the screen. Dimming cannot work without it
Accessibility service Optional Volume-button emergency shortcut, per-app dimming profiles, and dimming the notification panel

Night Mode does not need an account. The dimmer itself runs on the overlay permission alone, so you can leave Accessibility off and everything else still works.

Tips for using it at night¶

  • Combine Night Mode with Android’s dark mode so white apps do not glare
  • Lower the system brightness to its minimum first, then add Night Mode on top
  • Keep the notification enabled so the OFF button is always one tap away
  • Night Mode is a screen dimmer, not a medical device
