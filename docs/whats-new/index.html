What's new¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer

The latest version of Screen & Notification Dimmer is 8.0.2, released on Google Play on 21 November 2026. It targets Android 16 (API 36) and supports Android 8.0 and newer. It was first released publicly on 8 October 2026, and it has had more than 40 code changes since mid-September 2026. Most of them improve reliability across phone brands, make the permission screens clearer, and add ways to use Pro features for free.

November 2026¶

8.0.2 (21 November)¶

  • Rewarded ads wait for the ad to load instead of losing it, so "Watch an ad" works more reliably on slow connections. Ads now load only when you open an unlock sheet or tap the button.
  • Locked Pro switches, including Dim notification bar, have a "Watch ad" chip that plays the ad straight away.
  • Permission pages explain the Accessibility steps more clearly before sending you to Settings.
  • Notification bar titles wrap instead of being cut off; the edge slider's power button is now part of Pro.

8.0.1 and earlier November builds (18–21 November)¶

  • Unlock any Pro feature for 12 hours with one ad; three ads unlock all of Pro.
  • In-app updates through Google Play, so you get fixes sooner.
  • A lighter rendering mode that switches on by itself on slower phones.
  • Picture guides for each permission and a new Accessibility explanation page.
  • An "Ad privacy choices" row in Settings. The ad consent question is asked only on your first ad tap, or after three nights of use.
  • Analytics and Clarity only run once consent allows it, and the privacy text now matches exactly what the app does.
  • Fixed a start-up crash on some phones, and a crash when a screen was opened twice.

Early November¶

  • New logo and a large round of design and edge slider changes.
  • The app was renamed from Night Mode to Screen & Notification Dimmer. The app, your settings and the Google Play page stay the same.

October 2026¶

  • Public release on Google Play (8 October).
  • The edge slider became a Pro feature, alongside a reworked Pro page and a fixed home header.

September 2026¶

  • Google Play Billing and a one-screen Pro page (26 September).
  • A complete redesign: three tabs (Dim, Automate, Settings), shared components and new copy, plus the "Sunset" onboarding (22–24 September).
