Why Screen & Notification Dimmer?¶

By Madhav Raj, who builds Screen & Notification Dimmer · Updated 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer

Screen & Notification Dimmer is built around the parts of night-time phone use that ordinary dimmers miss. It can dim the notification shade, status bar and keyboard. You can change the darkness from the screen edge in any app. Auto-Dim catches bright pages, the bedtime schedule is free, and you can switch it off blind with the volume buttons. It is updated often; version 8.0.2 came out on 21 November 2026.

I'm Madhav Raj, and I build this app at Bunkwise Technologies in India. Here is the honest case for it, and where you might be better off with something else.

Six things it does differently¶

  1. It dims the parts other dimmers leave bright. The status bar, the pulled-down notification shade, Quick Settings, the keyboard and the lock screen. That is where the name comes from. How.
  2. The edge slider. Slide along the screen edge to change the darkness inside any app, like a volume bar. I didn't find this in the other dimmers I compared. How.
  3. Auto-Dim follows the content. It reacts when a white page opens, which a light sensor can't see in a dark room. How.
  4. The schedule is free. Several popular dimmers charge for it. How.
  5. You can't get stuck in the dark. Volume Up + Volume Down or a shake turns it off without looking, and the layer is capped at 97%. How.
  6. Pro without paying. One ad unlocks any Pro feature for 12 hours, and ads never appear on their own. How.

Actively maintained¶

Android changes the rules for overlays, background services and notifications almost every year. Dimmers that aren't updated tend to break quietly on new phones. This app gets frequent updates (see What's new), targets the latest Android version (API 36), and includes a checker for the battery settings that break dimmers on Xiaomi, Samsung and other brands.

Where other apps are better¶

  • No ads at all: Penumbra has none and no paywall.
  • Tasker automation: Penumbra and Twilight support it; I don't yet.
  • Sunset-following blue light filter: Twilight.
  • Root mode and customisable buttons: Darker.
  • Track record: Night screen and Twilight have millions of users. This app is new, with 1K+ downloads so far.
